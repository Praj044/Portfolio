PROJECT / 01SYSTEMS · C++20

LSM-Tree Key-Value Store

Built a persistent key-value store from scratch (~4.4K lines) with skip-list memtables, WAL, SSTables with a sparse index, Bloom filters, and size-tiered background compaction. Added CRC32 checksums and WAL replay to detect corruption and repair torn tails; validated with 44 unit tests and a 300K-operation mixed benchmark using four reader and two writer threads, with zero errors.
